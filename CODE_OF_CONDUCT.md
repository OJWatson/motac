# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, level of experience, or field of work.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful in language and tone
- giving and receiving constructive feedback
- assuming good intent while remaining open to correction
- focusing discussion on the work rather than the person
- being thoughtful about the limits of volunteer time and attention

## Unacceptable behaviour

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- sexualised language or unwanted sexual attention
- trolling, baiting, or sustained disruption of discussion
- publishing private information without consent
- any conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other public or private project communication channels.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it to the project maintainer. If direct contact is not practical, open a GitHub issue or discussion requesting a private follow-up without sharing sensitive details publicly.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation where necessary.
